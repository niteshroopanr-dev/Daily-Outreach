Inferred Commercial Goal

Catalytic IT appears to be moving from an organic, delivery led growth phase into a deliberately engineered sales and geographic expansion phase, funded so far entirely through operating performance, with no external raise or debt facility found anywhere in the public record.

This is inferred from two things read together. First, the business has stated a specific plan to build a brand new sales team over the next 12 months, per its own Smart50 2025 profile. Second, the delivery headcount shows almost no movement, 23 people cited in both the 2023 and 2025 Smart50 profiles, while revenue nearly tripled from 2.5 million to 7.4 million over the same period. That combination points to a business that has been productivity led, not headcount led, and is now about to add a genuinely different kind of cost, a sales function, on top of two new market entries, a Devonport, Tasmania office already open and a planned Kuala Lumpur entry.

This is an inference from verified public information about the business. It may not reflect the actual internal plan at Catalytic IT.
