Commercial Wedge

The Commercial Argument

Taskforce Australia has built something genuinely rare: 19 people coordinating 5,500 tradespeople, 140,000+ compliance and maintenance jobs, and a client roster spanning 20 consumer brands and more than 300 real estate offices. Three consecutive Smart50 appearances and a move from $9.7 million to $12.8 million in a single year confirm the momentum is real.

The commercial question that follows this growth is precise. Taskforce operates two structurally different revenue channels within one entity. RentSafe delivers compliance work for manufacturers and real estate offices on a per job basis. RentRepair, launched as Australia's only complete subscription maintenance service, brings a fixed monthly fee model aimed directly at rental providers. These two channels have fundamentally different economics: one is volume and frequency driven, the other is recurring revenue with customer lifetime value and churn dynamics. Until each client, across both channels, is ranked by gross margin contribution, revenue per job, and effort to serve, the business is navigating its next growth phase on revenue totals alone.

A Customer Concentration and Profitability Map answers this in three weeks. Every client ranked by revenue, gross margin contribution, and effort to serve. The output is a clear action list: which manufacturer accounts to grow, which real estate offices to reprice, and which subscription customers are on track to return value over their lifetime. For a business doubling its agency client base and launching a second revenue engine simultaneously, this is the foundational commercial intelligence for the next phase.
