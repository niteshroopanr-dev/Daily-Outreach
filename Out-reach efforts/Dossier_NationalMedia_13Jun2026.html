5.3 The Wedge and the Matched Service

Inferred Commercial Goal Inference, not verified fact

National Media is growing at 42 percent per year with a stated strategy (from Mark Harvey's public SISO CEO Summit presentation) of organic portfolio expansion through new event launches. The inferred commercial goal for the next 12 months is: continue building the event portfolio while managing the increasing complexity of running nine or more event brands across three states. The most likely financial management gap at this stage is the absence of a rigorous, event-by-event profitability picture. With nine or more brands, some events almost certainly cross-subsidise others. Without clarity on which events generate the real margin, capital allocation for new launches lacks an evidence base.

The Wedge

National Media has produced trade exhibitions across Australia since 1993. With $18.4 million in revenue, 48 staff, and nine or more active event brands spanning food and hospitality, workplace health and safety, construction, and accommodation technology, it has built a genuinely diverse B2B events portfolio.

The 42 percent three year average growth rate, verified by the SmartCompany Smart50 2025 award citation, places National Media among Australia's fastest-growing businesses. Mark Harvey's public commentary at the SISO CEO Summit on organic growth via event launches confirms that the strategy is to keep building the portfolio.

The commercial gap that typically opens at this stage in a multi-brand events business is between the portfolio's total revenue growth and the real margin generated by each individual event. At $18.4 million across nine or more brands, the aggregate number masks what is almost certainly a wide spread of unit economics. Some events will be delivering strong contribution margins. Others will be absorbing shared overhead from the profitable ones, or generating revenue that looks healthy before costs are properly allocated.

The Product and Service Line Profitability review addresses this directly. In three weeks, it ranks every event brand by gross margin, contribution margin, and operational drag. The output is a clear, evidence-based decision for each brand: scale, reprice, rationalise, or exit. For a leadership team actively allocating capital to new launches, that map is the most leveraged financial insight available.
