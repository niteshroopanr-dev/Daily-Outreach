Taskforce Australia operates at $674K revenue per employee, which places it in genuinely rare territory for a services business. The RentSafe platform has demonstrated clear product market fit and created a repeatable compliance revenue base with 500 real estate agency clients. The challenge is not identifying opportunities. The challenge is sequencing four competing priorities against a finite capital and team bandwidth, and building a growth plan that is fundable and executable rather than aspirational. Social housing expansion requires a different sales motion and contract structure than the existing real estate agency base. The staff equity plan has balance sheet and cashflow implications. The AI platform investment is a capital decision with a return horizon. Organic growth in the core business competes for the same team attention as all three of the above.

A Strategic Growth Diagnostic maps the revenue, capacity, and margin headroom of the current business, then produces a 12 month growth plan with funding and capital allocation steps spelled out across three scenarios.
