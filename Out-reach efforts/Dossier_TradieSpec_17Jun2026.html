The Commercial Argument

TradieSpec has raised $13 million and deployed it across a fleet of over 600 vehicles spanning Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Perth, Central Coast, and Newcastle. Revenue stands at $15.3 million with 22 people managing operations across six locations. The business has set a clear public goal: double the fleet within 12 months and become Australia's largest trade vehicle provider.

Before the next capital commitment is made, the single most valuable financial analysis available is a rigorous answer to where each dollar of the existing fleet is generating the highest return. Which markets lead on vehicle utilisation? Which vehicle categories (utes, vans, tippers) carry the strongest gross margin? Which customer segments convert from flexible hire to rent to own most reliably, and what does that mean for the capital cycle?

Without a formal capital allocation map, expansion risk concentrates in the deployment decision: the next city gets capital because it is the next city, not because the evidence supports it. The Capital Allocation Review is a focused engagement that maps every vehicle category and market against the revenue and margin it produces, ranks the redeployment options, and produces a clear investment thesis for the next fleet round. For a business growing at 57 percent with $13 million already deployed, this is the highest leverage financial insight available in the next 90 days.
