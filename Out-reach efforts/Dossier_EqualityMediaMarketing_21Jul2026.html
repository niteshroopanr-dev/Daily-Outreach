The Wedge and the Matched Service

Revenue moved from $7.3 million to $13 million between the 2024 and 2025 Smart50 profiles, a 78 percent increase, while the team roughly doubled from 15 to around 30 in about the same window. Headcount growth has therefore run ahead of revenue growth, which is exactly the pattern that quietly compresses margin in a scaling agency. At the same time the business is deliberately shifting mix, from a media buying practice into a full service model where about half of clients now also buy strategy and creative, three service lines with very different margin and staffing profiles. Nothing published shows which of the three, media, creative, or strategy, is actually compounding profit per person versus simply consuming the new capacity.
