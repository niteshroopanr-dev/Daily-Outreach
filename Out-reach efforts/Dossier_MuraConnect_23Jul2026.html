The wedge and the matched service

Revenue grew from $2.7 million to $10.1 million in three years, a 90 percent average annual rate that placed MuraConnect sixth nationally in the 2025 Smart50 Awards, while full time headcount doubled in the year to November 2023. That combination, rapid revenue growth alongside rapid headcount growth in a recruitment and IT delivery business, is exactly where cash visibility and management reporting tend to lag behind the trading result. The government client base is also broadening in a good way, departments served grew from 4 to 15 in a year and a New South Wales panel registration has followed the Queensland base, but public sector concentration remains a real feature of the revenue mix. With three co founders steering a business that has tripled in size, a monthly financial partner would give the leadership team cash flow visibility, margin discipline on placement and delivery work, and the board grade reporting rhythm that government clients and panel assessors already expect.
