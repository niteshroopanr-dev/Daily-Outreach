Inferred Commercial Goal

Taskforce has grown revenue from $7.43 million in 2023 to $12.8 million in 2025, a near doubling in two years, while the SmartCompany Smart50 citations show headcount held flat at 19 across the same period. That growth has come through three distinct product lines with three different economics: TradieConnect, the original project based warranty and installation work for consumer brands, RentSafe, compliance checking largely delivered under contract to real estate offices, and RentRepair, a flat $59 a month subscription launched to build a recurring revenue base.

The company has publicly stated a target of 50 to 60 percent revenue CAGR over the next three years, and has said that growth so far has been funded organically rather than through a further capital raise. Funding that pace of growth from operations, across three lines with different pricing models and cost structures, means every dollar of margin matters. The inferred goal for the next 12 months is to know, with certainty, which of the three lines is generating the actual margin that will fund the stated target, so that hiring, technology investment and account management effort are weighted toward the line that pays rather than spread evenly across all three.

This is an inference from verified public information about the business. It may not reflect the actual situation inside Taskforce.
