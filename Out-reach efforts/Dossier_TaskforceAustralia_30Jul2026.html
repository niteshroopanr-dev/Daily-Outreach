Inferred Commercial Goal

Taskforce has told SmartCompany it is targeting fifty to sixty percent compound annual growth over the next three years, funded entirely through organic means with no external capital. Revenue has already moved from $7.43 million in FY2023 to $12.8 million in FY2025 while the team has held near twenty people, a strong productivity result for a business that routes jobs through several thousand independent tradespeople to service real estate agencies nationally.

That model creates two separate payment clocks on every job: one for paying the tradesperson who completed it, and one for collecting from the real estate client who ordered it. When a self funded business commits publicly to accelerating growth, the gap between those two clocks becomes the de facto source of growth capital. The inferred goal for the next twelve months is therefore to convert cash currently trapped in that cycle, debtor terms, supplier and contractor payment terms, and any facility headroom, into fuel for the stated growth target, without needing to raise external funds.

This is an inference from verified public information about the business. It may not reflect the actual situation at Taskforce Australia.
