5.3 The Wedge and the Matched Service

Inferred commercial goal (inference, not a verified fact): fund and prove the unit economics of a multi site physical retail rollout, following three years of near ten times revenue growth as a pure online, direct to consumer brand.

Paire took a business from $1 million to $10 million in revenue between 2021 and 2024 while staying lean, sixteen full time staff, then made the leap in January 2025 from online only trading into its first flagship store at QV Melbourne. The next 12 to 18 months already carry a stated plan for two to three more stores in Sydney plus further Melbourne sites. That is the exact moment a lean online retailer's expansion plan needs to become a costed, scenario tested growth and capital plan rather than a series of individually funded openings, each judged on instinct rather than a shared model.

The Strategic Growth Diagnostic maps revenue, capacity and margin headroom across the existing and planned stores, then produces a 12 month growth plan with the funding and capital allocation steps for each new site spelled out, tested across base, downside and upside scenarios. The Capital Allocation Review then confirms where each dollar, whether from trading cashflow or capital raised through channels such as the 2024 Shark Tank pitch, is best deployed across new stores, stock holding for the wool blend range, and the Singapore expansion already underway.
