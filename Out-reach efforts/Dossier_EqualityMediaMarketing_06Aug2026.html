The Wedge and the Matched Service

Revenue reported at Smart50 award time nearly doubled in one year, from $7.3 million to $13 million, while headcount moved from 17 toward around 30. At the same time the agency took on a new ANZ media planning and buying mandate for BIODERMA. Full service media agencies typically pay media suppliers ahead of client settlement, so a growing account book widens the gap between cash going out and cash coming in. Combined with payroll scaling at a similar pace, this is a textbook setup for cash getting trapped inside growth rather than a profit problem. A focused working capital project, mapping debtors, supplier timing and any stock or work in progress, is the most direct lever available right now.
