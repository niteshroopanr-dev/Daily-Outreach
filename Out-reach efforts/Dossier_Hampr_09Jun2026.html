5.3   The Wedge and Matched Service
The Commercial Wedge

Hampr has grown its revenue to $12.7 million at 153% year on year while simultaneously pursuing enterprise contracts with extended payment terms and funding a market entry into Singapore. The business operates a marketplace model that handles payments between corporate clients and a network of food and event suppliers, creating float management complexity that grows directly with transaction volume. With 37 staff across five Australian cities and no publicly named CFO or Head of Finance, the financial architecture is almost certainly straining against the growth momentum.

The question is not whether a senior finance function is needed but how to add one without a full time overhead commitment at this stage. A Fractional CFO Partnership addresses this directly: a senior financial partner at the table on a monthly cadence, building the monthly management pack, the quarterly board grade review, and providing the ad hoc decision support Hampr needs to model the Singapore launch properly, manage marketplace cash flow, and land and service enterprise contracts with financial confidence.
