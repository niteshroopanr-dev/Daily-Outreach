The wedge and the matched service

Taskforce's revenue has grown from 7.43 million to 12.8 million dollars across FY2023 to FY2025 (SmartCompany Smart50 citations), while the publicly disclosed team has held near flat at 19 to 20 people across the same period. The business now coordinates over 5,000 licensed tradespeople against more than 140,000 jobs a year, across three distinct commercial lines: RentSafe compliance checks, the RentRepair subscription launched from 59 dollars a month, and manufacturer installation partnerships with brands including Brilliant Lighting and Universal Fans (Taskforce's own published material). Each of these lines likely carries a materially different margin profile and coordination cost, yet all three are scaling inside the same flat operating team.
