The Wedge and Matched Service

Attekus spent seven years building Bookable into the platform behind close to 20 percent of local councils across Australia and New Zealand, entirely without outside capital. In October 2024 that changed. Five V Capital led the business's first ever external raise, an AUD 5 million round joined by QIC Ventures, earmarked for entry into the United Kingdom local government market alongside expansion into Australian and New Zealand education, and state and federal government agencies. Three new markets, one pool of growth capital, and for the first time in the company's history, institutional shareholders expecting to see the return on every dollar deployed.

That is precisely the point at which a capital allocation review earns its keep. Ranking the United Kingdom entry, the education vertical and the government agency push by expected return before further capital is committed turns the next board conversation with Five V Capital and QIC Ventures into one grounded in a documented redeployment plan, not founder instinct.
