The Wedge And The Matched Service

Orbitkey has funded six Kickstarter campaigns worth USD 3.25 million to bring new products to market and is running a seventh campaign now, while operating five third party logistics warehouses to service exports to more than 100 countries and over 1,000 stockists. That pattern, funding new production runs by pre selling to backers rather than from working capital the business generates itself, is common in fast growing physical product brands, and it usually means cash is trapped somewhere in the cycle between paying suppliers, holding stock across five warehouses, and collecting from wholesale accounts on terms.

At $24.6 million of revenue and 20% three year average growth, the business has clearly outgrown ad hoc production financing. A Working Capital Unlock maps exactly where cash is trapped across debtors, inventory, and supplier terms across the whole multi warehouse operation, and produces a prioritised list to release it, so the next product launch can be funded from the business rather than from the next crowdfunding campaign.
