The wedge and the matched service

Inference, not a verified fact: Sententia's likely goal over the next 12 months is to build the financial planning and capital allocation discipline that lets it keep scaling across two states without margin or delivery slipping, and to actively manage the concentration sitting inside its Commonwealth contract book.

Sententia is compounding at a pace few professional services firms sustain: a 60.2 percent three year revenue CAGR (AFR Fast 100 2025, rank 45), a jump from two founders in March 2020 to a near 20 strong team by 2023, and a second state office opened in Melbourne in August 2024 under its own Managing Director. Federal contract disclosures tracked by Pollywatch put lifetime Commonwealth contract value at $45.3 million across 174 notices, with more than half of that concentrated in two agency relationships, Services Australia and the Department of Health and Aged Care.

That combination, rapid multi state growth plus a contract book concentrated in a handful of large government relationships, is the profile of a business whose delivery capacity has likely outpaced its financial planning and capital allocation discipline. A Strategic Growth Diagnostic gives Sententia a costed 12 month plan, mapping revenue, capacity and margin headroom against its two office model, with funding and capital allocation steps spelled out before the next hiring or office decision is made on instinct rather than a plan.
