Inferred Commercial Goal

AdUnion has compounded at 81 percent over three years and sits at $9.3 million in revenue with ten people, making it one of the leanest high growth agencies in Australia. Its founders built their edge in streaming media, not in financial management, and the business has added client diversity rapidly across retail, telco, travel, financial services, and FMCG through the AdMatch platform advantage.

At this velocity and client mix, the inferred goal for the next 12 months is to understand which accounts are genuinely profitable after the full cost of service, team time, and technology investment are allocated. Revenue per person at $930K suggests very high gross productivity, but gross revenue does not tell the full story: in an agency with proprietary technology investment, some accounts likely subsidise others without the team knowing. With Adam Shalagin now leading AdMatch separately and Robert Ong steering AdUnion as a standalone agency, the commercial question shifts from growth to profitable growth. Knowing which clients to grow, which to reprice, and which to reset is the most valuable financial insight at this stage.

This is an inference from verified public information about the business. It may not reflect the actual situation at AdUnion.
