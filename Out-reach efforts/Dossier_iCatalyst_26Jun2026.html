The Wedge and Matched Service
Commercial Argument

iCatalyst generates over 70% of its $13.5M revenue from time-billed consultant work, confirmed by the Microsoft Customer Stories case study. With 50 consultants now operating across Perth, Melbourne, Sydney, and Brisbane, and the Beyond CRM team freshly integrated in June 2025, the founders are running a utilisation-dependent business at scale without a mapped baseline for revenue per head or billable mix across offices. At $13.5M across 50 staff, the business is generating strong revenue overall. The risk at this stage is not topline growth but whether that revenue is distributed evenly across the national team, or whether certain practices or locations are carrying the load while others dilute margin as they ramp. The Workforce Capacity and Utilisation Review maps billable mix, revenue per full time employee, and capacity headroom across all four offices and all service lines, giving the founders an evidence base before the next round of east coast hiring. This is the exact decision point the founders face now: before signing the next 5 to 10 consultants across Melbourne, Sydney, and Brisbane, they need to know where the existing 50 are generating value and where the capacity sits.
