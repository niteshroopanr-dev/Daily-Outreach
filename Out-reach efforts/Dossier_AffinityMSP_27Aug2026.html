Inferred Commercial Goal

Affinity MSP has compounded revenue 42 percent over three years to $6.6 million with a team of 36, and has just been named the top ranked Australian managed service provider on the 2026 Channel Futures MSP 501 index, 69th globally. The award panel explicitly weighs financial performance and operational depth, so this recognition is a strong external signal that the business is executing well.

The inferred goal for the next 12 months is protecting margin through the next phase of headcount growth. Managed service providers are a classically utilisation based business: revenue depends on the ratio of billable technician time to total paid capacity. As MSPs scale past the 30 to 40 person mark, it is common for that ratio to quietly erode, because onboarding, support tickets, and non billable overhead absorb capacity faster than pricing and hiring plans account for. The business has also grown without external investors and has reportedly chosen not to pursue mergers, which is admirable discipline but also means every capital and pricing decision runs through a founder led team with no dedicated finance function testing the numbers independently.

This is an inference from verified public information about the business. It may not reflect the actual situation inside Affinity MSP.
