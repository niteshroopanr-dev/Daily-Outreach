The wedge and the matched service

Inferred commercial goal (inference, not a verified fact): iCatalyst appears to be scaling delivery headcount and leadership faster than any public evidence of a matching investment in operational visibility. This is a classic strain point for a professional services firm mid build phase, right after taking on a second Dynamics practice through the Beyond CRM acquisition. The inference rests on the pace of hiring (two senior Delivery Executives in one month), the national footprint expansion, and the acquisition, not on any disclosed internal metric.

The commercial argument: iCatalyst has more than doubled revenue in two years while growing from 33 to 50 people, then layered on two senior Delivery Executives specifically to run larger projects, then absorbed a second practice through acquisition. Every one of those moves is the right instinct individually. Together they raise the question every scaling consultancy eventually faces: is billable time across a now multi state, multi practice team converting into margin as cleanly as headcount and acquisition cost are converting into overhead. Nobody has publicly said iCatalyst has a utilisation problem; the pattern simply says it is time to check.
