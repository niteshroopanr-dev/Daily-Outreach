The Wedge and the Matched Service

TMC now holds moissanite and lab grown diamond stock across four showrooms on three continents, opened in roughly eighteen months, while staking close to a third of its annual revenue on a single BFCM week that is planned six to eight months out. That is a working capital design problem as much as a growth story. Every new showroom and every pre BFCM stock build locks up cash before it converts to a sale, and four sites multiply that exposure at once. A structured working capital project maps exactly where that cash sits, from showroom stock to workshop inventory to supplier terms, and gives Makayla and Tom a release plan sized to the same pace of growth they are already executing.
