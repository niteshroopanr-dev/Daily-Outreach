The Wedge

National Media has executed a textbook roll-up in the B2B events sector: three acquisition programmes in roughly 18 months have doubled revenue to $18.4 million and stretched the portfolio to 13 national events across 10 distinct industry brands. That is an impressive pace of external growth. The tension it creates is equally clear. A 48-person team is now responsible for integrating multiple acquired portfolios simultaneously, running a national event calendar, managing show-level P and Ls across wildly different sectors, from aged care catering to fitness to architecture, and deciding which acquisitions to pursue next.

The Finance Director (Holly Doughty) is in place to handle operational finance. What National Media at $18.4 million, in active acquisition mode, genuinely needs is a senior strategic CFO voice at the boardroom level: someone who can model acquisition economics before a deal is signed, build the capital allocation framework across 10 brands, stress test integration scenarios, and produce the board-grade reporting that tells Brad Langton exactly where the portfolio margin is coming from and where it is leaking. That is a distinct capability from operational finance, and it is precisely what ProfitPulse's Fractional CFO Partnership delivers at a fixed monthly fee.
